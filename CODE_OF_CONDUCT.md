# Code of conduct

PawPal contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful,
specific, and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that do
not meet these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through a contact
channel listed on the maintainer's repository-host profile. Reports will be
reviewed with appropriate confidentiality and fairness.
